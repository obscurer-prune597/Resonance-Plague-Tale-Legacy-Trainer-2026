## 🎮 About the Game

Released on August 27, 2026, Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is a third-person action-adventure game developed by Asobo Studio and published by Focus Entertainment. Set 15 years before A Plague Tale: Requiem, it follows Sophia as she explores the mysterious Minotaur's Island, uncovers ancient secrets, and confronts a long-buried curse.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life and combat enhancements for Sophia's journey. It supports the game's melee combat, parries, attack combos, stealth encounters, grappling-hook interactions, exploration, and environmental puzzles while allowing players to customize difficulty and resource management.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to reduce combat difficulty, experiment with Sophia's melee and grappling-hook abilities, streamline exploration, or replay the story with more flexible resource management.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the game and load a save before activating movement-related options. Use the movement-speed feature to adjust traversal across the island, but disable it during scripted sequences, precise platforming, or puzzle interactions if movement behaves unexpectedly.

### Tools & Inventory

Activate unlimited resources after loading into gameplay. The option preserves available collected items for continued use, making it easier to experiment with equipment and supplies without repeatedly searching the island. Disable the feature before opening shops, crafting interfaces, or saving if the game build shows inventory irregularities.

### World & Environment

Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy combines exploration, dangerous trails, environmental hazards, stealth encounters, and light-manipulation puzzles across the Minotaur's Island and its Minoan-era sequences. Use health protection and stealth assistance to explore safely, while keeping puzzle assistance disabled when you want to solve the island's mechanisms normally.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate combat options only after gaining direct control of Sophia. Infinite health provides protection during difficult encounters, while the damage multiplier and one-hit defeats speed up fights against regular enemies. The perfect-parry option helps with precise defensive timing, and enhanced grappling-hook functionality supports pulling enemies closer or sending them into hazards. Disable aggressive combat options before boss transitions or major scripted scenes.
